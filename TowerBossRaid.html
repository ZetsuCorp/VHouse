VHOUSE

TOWER BOSS RAID

Game Design Notes

1. Concept

Tower Boss Raid is a VHouse battle mode inspired by Mortal Kombat's tower battles, using a simplified, automated survival system.

Players send VCards into timed raids to challenge bosses, attack the RAID EGG, and earn rewards.

2. Index Integration

  • A Tower Boss Raid button appears in the background of the Index map.
  • The button opens a dedicated Tower Boss Raid page.

3. Player Side

  1. Player selects Tower Boss Raid.
  2. Player selects a Raid Level.
  3. Player selects a VCard.
  4. Player pays the $0.75 raid entry fee.
  5. The VCard is locked during the raid timer, similar to the Journey system.
  6. The raid runs using its own movement animation.
  7. When the timer ends, the VCard returns with its raid results and applicable rewards.

4. Raid Side

  1. Each Raid Level is one stage with 10 Boss encounters.
  2. The VCard enters the selected Raid Level.
  3. Boss encounters use Zetsu Counter Blitz rules.
  4. Winning allows movement to the next Boss encounter.
  5. Losing ends that raid attempt.

5. Zetsu Counter Blitz Battle Rules

  • Player and Boss alternate turns.
  • Each turn uses a coin flip generated from secure random bytes hashed with SHA-256.
  • HEADS = 1 successful hit; TAILS = miss.
  • First participant to score 3 hits wins.
  • Winning a Boss fight permits the VCard to attack the RAID EGG.

These calculations happen automatically in the background. Combat is presented through a small movement animation rather than manual controls.

6. VCard ATK and DEF

ATK and DEF derive from the player's total Scrap holdings.

  • ATK = Total Scraps / 2
  • DEF = Total Scraps / 2
  • ATK + DEF = Total Scrap balance

The combined ATK and DEF determine the balance used to calculate RAID EGG health.

ATK is also the amount of damage dealt to the RAID EGG after a successful Boss battle.

7. RAID EGG

The RAID EGG is one shared health bar covering all 10 Boss encounters in the selected Raid Level.

  • Each successful Boss fight permits one attack against the RAID EGG.
  • The VCard's ATK is subtracted from the remaining RAID EGG health.
  • RAID EGG health is based on the player's combined ATK and DEF.

Raid Levels

  • Level 1: RAID EGG HP = (ATK + DEF) × 5
  • Level 2: RAID EGG HP = (ATK + DEF) × 10
  • Level 3: RAID EGG HP = (ATK + DEF) × 15

Raid Level 1 Calculation

RAID EGG HP = (ATK + DEF) × 5

Example

VCard ATK: 2
VCard DEF: 2
Total Scrap balance: 4
Raid Level: 1
RAID EGG HP: (2 + 2) × 5 = 20

After winning a Boss fight:
VCard attacks RAID EGG for 2 damage.
Remaining RAID EGG HP: 18

8. Difficulty and Rewards

Tower Boss Raid difficulty scales with the player's Scrap holdings.

  • More Scraps means higher ATK and DEF.
  • Higher combined stats increase RAID EGG health.
  • Higher RAID EGG health increases the challenge.
  • Greater difficulty provides greater rewards.

The purpose is to reward players for holding more Scraps while requiring greater raid performance to earn those rewards.

9. Entry Fees, Retries, and Rewards

  • Raid entry fee: $0.75.
  • The initial attempt is included with entry.
  • One retry after defeat is free.
  • Each additional retry costs $0.50.
  • Each defeated Boss earns $0.15–$0.25.
  • A Raid Level has 10 Boss encounters per attempt.

10. Baseline Boss Encounter Odds

  • Chance of winning one Boss encounter: 56.79%
  • Chance of defeating all 10 Bosses: 0.35% (about 1 in 287)
  • Chance with the free retry: 0.70% (about 1 in 144)

These baseline figures do not include Lucky Stone retries.

11. Lucky Stone and Creation Unit

  • LUCKY STONE is a separate item from Journey's LUCK.
  • It works the same way: held Lucky Stones provide additional coin-flip retries after a loss.
  • Lucky Stone has a limit of 5 and costs more than regular LUCK.
  • The Creation Unit in the Shop supplies additional ATK to help break the RAID EGG.
  • The Creation Unit has not been built yet.

12. Progressive RAID EGG Bet

  • At the start of each Raid Level, the player can optionally donate to the RAID EGG.
  • Each encounter adds 0.25× to the Egg reward multiplier, starting at 0×.
  • The player must break the RAID EGG to collect the multiplied Egg reward.
  • The Egg Bet is separate from the $0.15–$0.25 earned for each defeated Boss.
Example: $5.00 Egg Bet

10 encounters × 0.25× = 2.50× multiplier.

$5.00 × 2.50 = $12.50 payout if the Egg is broken.
