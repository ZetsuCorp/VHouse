VHouse goes beyond the traditional NFT earning model by giving players a way to push value into each NFT created and used within VHouse.

While you level up and grow your NFT monetarily, increases in the price of SOL also increase the dollar value of the SOL-backed portion of your collection.

WHAT IS A VCARD?

A VCard is an NFT on the Solana blockchain. A Soulbound, customizable, upgradable, monetizable NFT that grows in value multiple ways because of VHouse.

📈LEVEL
⚔️STATS
📜HISTORY
💰VALUE

The VCard holds an NFT's level, stats, gameplay history, and monetary value. A player can have multiple VCards, building a treasure of working NFTs!

GROWING AN NFT

VHouse LOAD allows you to bring OLD NFTs you already own into the game.

Transform static digital collectibles into active in-game characters that can participate in VHouse activities and earn real-money rewards.

🖼️ 1. LOAD

LOAD an NFT from your connected Phantom wallet.

🛡️ 2. TEAM

Choose its VHouse TEAM and position its artwork.

🃏 3. MINT

MINT your new VCard to bring it into VHouse.

⚔️ 4. PLAY

Use your VCard in VHouse activities to pursue rewards and grow its in-game value.

Your original NFT remains yours and is never modified. Your new VCard gives it a separate playable identity within VHouse.

THREE MAIN WAYS TO PLAY AND EARN

💰 Monetary Growth +$0.25

Push value into your NFT through Missions, Journeys, and Raids! Each mission adds $0.25 into your active VCard.

⚔️ Character Growth ATK / DEF

Rewards are not just items. They are both Crypto and NFTs that have real market value while increasing your VCard's ATK and DEF.

📈 Market Growth SOL ↑

Just like any normal NFT, as SOL increases in price, the SOL-backed value of your NFT increases! Which means even more rewards on burn.

🔥 VCard Burn

Burning a VCard returns to your wallet all the money earned while interacting with VHouse using the VCard you're removing from play.

MISSION JOURNEYS

Send your VCards on Journeys to explore different Stages, complete encounters and earn rewards.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Select your VCard and choose a Stage.
  2. Equip available items to improve your chances.
  3. Complete each HUNT or FIGHT encounter.
  4. Earn VCard Cash, Scrap tokens, SOL and $AWT depending on the Stage.
  5. Review and confirm your Journey receipt to collect your rewards.

Multiple VCards can travel on separate Journeys simultaneously.
