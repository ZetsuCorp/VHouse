GAME RULES

TURN ORDER

  1. Roll the D12.
  2. Move your active Pokémon to the numbered square.
  3. Activate the TOP effect.
  4. Activate the BOTTOM effect.
  5. Activate the MIDDLE effect, if applicable.
  6. End the turn.

Both Player and CPU follow the same turn order.

FREEZE, PARALYZED, and SLEEP prevent the affected Pokémon's next action. The previous square does not activate again.

TRAINER & POKÉMON RULES

FAINT: A Pokémon faints at 3 HITS.

HITS: Each Pokémon keeps its own HIT count.

STATUS: The banner shows the active Pokémon's condition.

SWAPPING: Incoming Pokémon retain their HITS and STATUS.

TM RKT: A trapped Pokémon remains in the TM RKT spot. Landing on TEAM ROCKET ROLL again gives a recovery attempt. EVEN succeeds; ODD fails.

FLY & DIG

  1. Land on a FLY or DIG square.
  2. Resolve its effects.
  3. Remain inactive for one turn.
  4. Move to the matching destination.
COLLISION: If both Pokémon land on the same square, move them to squares 5 and 8 without activating those squares again.
