CRYPTO SYSTEM 01

Scraps — Creator Holder Rewards

Creator rewards tokens can be shared with wallets or charities from the coin page after creation.

For Creator Holders, 100% of creator rewards go to holders. Anyone holding more than $20 worth of the coin qualifies.

The goal is to reward holders after Journeys. These rewards are called Scraps.

Two Purposes of Scraps

  1. Scraps function as rewards tokens.
  2. Scrap token holdings determine the ATK and DEF values of VCards.

Battle Point Calculation

The total Scrap balance is divided equally between ATK and DEF.

FORMULA

ATK = Total Scrap Balance / 2

DEF = Total Scrap Balance / 2

Last Earned Snapshots

ATK and DEF are stored as last-earned snapshots. This avoids checking the player's Scrap token balance every second.

REWARD LOOP

Complete Journey → Earn Scraps → Increase Scrap Balance → Increase ATK & DEF → Stronger VCard

CRYPTO SYSTEM 02

Raid Boss & Battle Points

ATK and DEF are called Battle Points. These values are used during Raid Events.

Raid Structure

  1. Raid Events occur randomly throughout the month.
  2. Collect Raid entry fees.
  3. Register the list of participating players.
  4. Collect the list of participating VCards.
  5. Set the Raid Boss timer.
  6. Run 10 rounds of increasingly stronger RPS attacks.
  7. Reduce each VCard's Battle Points according to the rounds.
  8. Calculate survival results.
  9. Those surviving all 10 rounds are paid the entry fee.

CRYPTO SYSTEM 03

Z Creation Unit

The Z Creation Unit allows players to create items through liquidity provision. It gives purpose to the Wind, Blade, and Stone attributes.

Creation Process

  1. The player pays the SOL, $AWT, or Scrap equivalent for entry.
  2. The game grants entry to the Creation Unit.
  3. The player selects a liquidity pool: Wind, Blade, or Stone.
  4. The player buys liquidity for item creation.
  5. The player receives LP tokens.
  6. Holding LP tokens provides in-game advantages.
  7. The player can reclaim liquidity by returning LP tokens, losing the associated item power.

Liquidity & Item Power

CORE PRINCIPLE

The liquidity pool operates normally, producing LP tokens.

VHouse assigns in-game effects to those tokens because the player holds them.

These effects are useful during Raid Events.

When liquidity is reclaimed through the return of LP tokens, the associated item loses power.

Exact boost values, LP token calculations, and detailed Creation Unit mechanics remain to be defined.
