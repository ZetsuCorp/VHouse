BRING YOUR CHARACTER TO LIFE IN VHOUSE

Create your own VCard and take it into the game!

MINT turns the character you create into a new Token-2022 VCard NFT connected to your wallet and VHouse. Your VCard becomes a playable character you can manage in your Closet and use in Journeys and other VHouse activities, with opportunities to earn rewards.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Choose your VHouse TEAM: STONE, WIND, or BLADE.
  2. Name your VCard, choose or upload its artwork, and position or crop the image.
  3. Add a character description and review your VCard preview.
  4. Press MINT and approve the transaction in Phantom.
  5. After verification, view your receipt and find your new VCard in your VHouse Closet.

UPGRADE YOUR VCARD

Your VCard is already alive! Now let's make it your own.

UPGRADE — $0.75

Change your VCard's name, team, artwork, or character description.

CUSTOMIZE — $0.75

Enhance your VCard's appearance with special visual effects and artwork positioning.

LEVEL UP — +1 LEVEL

Every successful UPGRADE or CUSTOMIZE adds one permanent level to your NFT, displayed directly on your VCard. Leveling up is included in the operation at no additional charge.

GROW YOUR VCARD'S VALUE

Every UPGRADE or CUSTOMIZE costs $0.75, which includes a $0.50 VHouse service fee and a $0.25 SOL deposit directly into your NFT, increasing its recoverable SOL balance through VHouse BURN.

Your VCard retains its original mint address and ownership.

LOAD EXISTING NFT

BRING YOUR NFTs TO LIFE IN VHOUSE

Turn your existing NFTs into playable, earning VCards!

VHouse LOAD allows you to bring NFTs you already own from Metaplex Core or Token-2022 into the game. Transform static digital collectibles into active in-game characters that can participate in VHouse activities and earn real-money rewards.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. LOAD an NFT from your connected Phantom wallet.
  2. Choose its VHouse TEAM and position its artwork.
  3. MINT your new VCard to bring it into VHouse.
  4. Use your VCard in VHouse activities to pursue rewards and grow its in-game value.

Your original NFT remains yours and is never modified. Your new VCard gives it a separate playable identity within VHouse.

BURN VCARD — RECLAIM SOL

VIEW SOLANA TRANSACTION

HOW IT WORKS

  1. PAY SERVICE FEE — $3 worth of SOL transfers from your Phantom wallet to the VHouse treasury.
  2. BURN NFT — BURN only destroys the new Token-2022 VCard created by VHouse.
  3. CLOSE TOKEN ACCOUNT — The SOL remaining in the NFT's token account returns to your Phantom wallet.
  4. CLOSE MINT ACCOUNT — The SOL stored in the NFT's mint account, including recoverable deposits from UPGRADE/CUSTOMIZE, returns to your Phantom wallet.
  5. TRANSACTION COMPLETES — The wallet's net balance reflects the service fee, recovered SOL, and network fee.
