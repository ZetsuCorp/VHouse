WELCOME TO VHOUSE

Your Journey Begins Here

VHouse is a DeFi adventure game where you create NFT-backed characters called VCards, send them on journeys, discover rewards, and level up.

Your ultimate goal is to Level up and Go on Journey Missions to collect 5 Totem Pages and assemble a Z Book, The Legendary NFT binder.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. 1. Explore the Town — Discover the VHouse, Journey, and Shop.
  2. 2. Create Your VCard — Visit VHouse, sign in with Discord, link your wallet, and use a picture to create an NFT-backed character.
  3. 3. Go on Journeys — Send your VCard on timed missions to search for items and face opposition.
  4. 4. Collect Rewards — Find scrap, in-game money, and potentially crypto, NFTs, and Totem Pages.
  5. 5. Grow Stronger — Level up your VCards, improve their stats, and explore tougher stages.

Remember: A VCard is temporarily unavailable while on a Journey. When it returns, VHouse records its results and rewards.

Take a look around town. When you're ready, go to VHouse to create your first VCard!
