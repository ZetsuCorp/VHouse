Lucky Charm

600 IGM

Each Lucky Charm provides 1 Lucky Retry and +1% to player stats while held in inventory. Players may hold a maximum of 5 Lucky Charms, providing up to 5 retries and +5%. Using a retry consumes one charm, reducing its quantity and stat bonus.

1 Lucky Retry +1% per Charm Maximum: 5
🥾

Swift Boots

1,200 IGM

Each pair of Swift Boots held in inventory increases the player's SPEED by 1. Using a pair reduces Journey time by 15 seconds. Only one pair may be used per Stage. Once used, the boots are consumed and SPEED decreases by 1.

+1 SPEED −15 Seconds 1 Use per Stage
🏮

Hunt Lantern

1,800 IGM

Nullifies the Stage's RPS advantage for one encounter, once. The lantern is consumed after use.

Nullifies RPS Advantage 1 Encounter Single Use
🎟️

Stage Ticket

2,400 IGM

Allows the player to enter a Stage without paying the Stage entry fee. One ticket is consumed for each free Stage entry.

Free Stage Entry 1 Ticket per Entry
